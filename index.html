Welcome to SENSULU

Sen Sulu is a Kazakhstan decorative cosmetics brand. The brand started in Astana in 2023. The name "Sen Sulu" means "you are beautiful" in Kazakh. The brand believes that every woman is already beautiful.

Our Philosophy

The symbol of Sen Sulu is harmony. It represents a balance between strength and tenderness. The brand wants women to feel confident. Sen Sulu focuses on both inner and outer beauty.

Brand Journey

Sen Sulu began its journey in 2023. The brand was founded in the city of Astana. Today, its products are available throughout Kazakhstan. Sen Sulu is officially represented in 11 countries.

What We Offer

Sen Sulu offers a wide range of decorative cosmetics for face, lips, eyes, and brows. The collection includes different beauty products for everyday makeup. Prices vary depending on the product. Visitors can explore the full collection on the Products page.
